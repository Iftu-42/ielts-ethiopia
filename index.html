What services does IELTS Ethiopia offer?

We offer 1-on-1 IELTS coaching, mock and evaluation packages, individual skill tests and a free monthly IELTS session.

When are mock exams available?

Mock and evaluation bookings are available on Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Results are provided within 24 hours.

Are the reported band scores official IELTS results?

No. Our scores are estimated IELTS-style results intended for preparation and performance assessment.

Choose a package, fill in your personal details, and submit the form. We’ll be in touch with you via Telegram.
